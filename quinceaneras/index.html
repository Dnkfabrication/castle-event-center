Court, Coach & Castle

A Quinceañera She Won't Stop Talking About

A quinceañera calls for a setting as big as the occasion, and a castle does that without trying. The Great Hall serves as an elegant event hall for the presentation, the father-daughter waltz, and the reception that follows, holding 100–125 guests with a dance floor built into the package and unlimited outdoor seating on the grounds for extended family and friends. The Cinderella coach sits just outside the doors, waiting for the photos every quinceañera album needs. Three ready rooms give the birthday girl and her court a private space to finish hair, makeup, and dresses before the presentation, and the working fountains and stone walls make a backdrop no photographer has to work hard to use.

Built for the Traditions

Your Traditions, Your Way

Bring your own DJ, your own decorations, and your own caterer — the kitchen is set up for outside catering so families can serve traditional dishes exactly the way they want them. Food and alcohol are both permitted for the family celebration. Court of honor entrances, the father-daughter waltz, and the changing of shoes all have room to happen the way they're supposed to, with the dance floor and the Great Hall's open layout built for a program, not just a party.

Good to Know

Quinceañera FAQ

How many guests can attend a quinceañera at the Castle?

100–125 guests indoors in the Great Hall, plus unlimited outdoor seating on the grounds.

Can we bring our own DJ and decorations?

Yes — the Great Hall is yours to decorate and staff as your family chooses.

Is there room for a full court and a choreographed dance?

Yes, the dance floor and open layout of the Great Hall are built for a program with a court of honor, not just a seated dinner.

What does a quinceañera package cost?

Packages run $750–$2,500 by tier and day of week — call for a quote based on your date and guest count.
