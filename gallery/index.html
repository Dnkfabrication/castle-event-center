A Look Inside and Around the Grounds

Pictures help, but the castle photographs differently depending on the event. Below is a mix of ceremonies on the lawn, receptions in the Great Hall, and the details that make weddings, quinceañeras, corporate banquets, and celebrations of life look different from anywhere else in Lubbock. The Great Hall works as a single event hall for all of it — suits of armor and stone walls for a reception, an open floor for a quinceañera's court and dance, tables set for a corporate banquet or a family reunion, or a quieter arrangement for a memorial reception. Call to schedule an in-person tour; the photos don't do the grounds justice.

Want to see it for yourself? Book a tour and walk the grounds before your event — most families say the photos only tell half the story. Package pricing is on the Packages page, and event-specific details are on the Weddings, Quinceañeras, Corporate Events, and Celebrations of Life pages.
