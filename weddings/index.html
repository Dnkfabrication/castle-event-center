A Wedding Venue in Lubbock Built Like a Castle

Lubbock couples choose Castle Event Center because no other wedding venue in West Texas looks like this one. Say your vows on the lawn against real castle walls and working fountains, then move your reception into the Great Hall beneath suits of armor and string lighting. Three ready rooms keep the wedding party comfortable while everyone gets ready, a nursery keeps the littlest guests happy during the reception, and the Cinderella coach is waiting outside for photographs you can't take anywhere else in the region. Indoor capacity runs 100–125 guests, and outdoor seating on the grounds is unlimited, so the ceremony and reception can be as large or as intimate as you need.

Before, During & After the Wedding

More Than the Big Day

A wedding weekend rarely starts and ends on the day itself. Many brides book their bridal shower here before the wedding, filling the Great Hall with the same castle backdrop for a smaller, daytime celebration. Rehearsal dinners start at $500 for up to three hours the night before. And couples come back — an anniversary party in the same hall where you said your vows is one of the more common repeat bookings we get, whether it's a first anniversary or a twenty-fifth.

Good to Know

Wedding FAQ

Can we get married outdoors and hold the reception indoors?

Yes — most weddings say vows on the lawn against the castle walls, then move guests into the Great Hall for the reception.

Is alcohol allowed?

Yes. Food and alcohol may be brought onto the property, and the venue has a full kitchen for outside catering.

How far ahead should we book?

Popular Saturday dates fill up months out, especially during spring and fall wedding season — call as soon as you have a date in mind.

Do you host rehearsal dinners?

Yes, rehearsal dinners start at $500 for up to three hours the night before your wedding.
