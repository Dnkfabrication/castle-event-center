Straightforward Pricing

Every Package, Explained

Every package includes the Great Hall, ready rooms, restrooms, and a full kitchen set up for outside catering — the difference between tiers is table and chair count, hours, linens, and how much setup is done before your guests arrive. Saturday and weekday rates differ, veterans receive 10% off any package, and a booking deposit secures your date once you're ready to move forward. These four packages cover wedding receptions, but the same event hall books just as often for quinceañeras, corporate banquets, family reunions, christmas parties, baby showers, bridal showers, anniversary parties, and celebrations of life — the pricing structure is the same regardless of the occasion.
