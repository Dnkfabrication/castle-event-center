Meetings, Banquets & Company Gatherings

An Event Hall With Room to Work and Room to Celebrate

Not every company event calls for a hotel conference room. Castle Event Center works as a full event hall for company banquets, employee appreciation dinners, award ceremonies, retirement send-offs, and holiday parties, with the Great Hall seating 100–125 guests indoors and unlimited outdoor space on the grounds for a lunch break or a mixer. Every December, local businesses book the hall for their annual christmas party — string lighting and stone walls do more for the mood than a strip-mall banquet room ever will. The kitchen is set up for outside catering, so lunch, dinner, or a plated banquet meal can come from whichever caterer your office already trusts.

Family Reunions & Community Gatherings

The Castle Isn't Only for Companies

Extended families use the same Great Hall for a family reunion that has outgrown anyone's backyard — enough tables and chairs for every branch of the family tree, a kitchen for a potluck or a hired caterer, and grounds big enough for kids to run while the adults catch up. Church groups, nonprofits, and community organizations book the same space for banquets, fundraisers, and end-of-year celebrations.
