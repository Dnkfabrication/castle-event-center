Space to Gather, Space to Grieve

A Memorial Reception Away From a Funeral Home

Not every family wants to gather at a funeral home for the reception after a service. Castle Event Center hosts a memorial reception or a celebration of life in the Great Hall, a quiet indoor space that seats 100–125 guests, with unlimited outdoor seating on the grounds for families who'd rather gather under open sky. The kitchen is set up for outside catering, so a reception meal can be handled by whichever caterer or family member the family trusts, and food is welcome on the property. Weekday rates are lower than weekend rates, which tends to suit a memorial reception better than a Saturday wedding schedule, and the grounds — fountains, stone walls, open lawn — offer a calmer backdrop than a hotel conference room.

Flexible, Private & Close to Home

Room to Plan on Short Notice

Families planning a celebration of life are often doing it on short notice, and the Castle can usually accommodate a date within a few weeks. The property is private — no other event sharing the grounds that day — and close to Lubbock, Wolfforth, and the surrounding communities, so out-of-town family doesn't have far to travel. Parking is on site, and the layout keeps the reception separate from where a formal service might be held, so families can choose whichever combination fits their plans.

Good to Know

Celebration of Life FAQ

Can Castle Event Center host a celebration of life or memorial reception?

Yes — the Great Hall and grounds are available for memorial receptions and celebrations of life, indoors or outdoors.

Is catering available?

Yes, the venue has a full kitchen and outside catering is welcome.

Can we book on short notice or during the week?

Usually yes — weekday rates are lower than weekend rates, and we do our best to accommodate short timelines. Call to check the calendar.

Is alcohol required?

No. Alcohol is permitted but not required — the venue is used both ways.
